## E2E Impact Notes (CASA-specific)
- **GL / Financial Postings** — impacted by batch transactions, disbursement, waivers, OD write-off, inward/outward clearance
- **Downstream Lending** — Rental Recovery Process draws from CASA; Lending disbursement payments post to CASA
- **EOD/Batch** — impacted by savings automation, process schedule setup, batch type definitions, standing order processing
- **Card/ATM** — card issuance and ATM transactions are downstream of account activation
- **Clearing** — inward and outward clearance are key upstream touchpoints for cheque-based transactions
- **KYC/COB** — account opening is downstream of customer onboarding; signature upload precedes account activation
